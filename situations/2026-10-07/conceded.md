# Goals against, matched with Champion 1 and above

Sessions: 2026-10-07. Every goal we conceded is walked back 0.5 s at a time for up to 10 s (or to the kickoff). At each step our position — the ball, our three cars and theirs, where each is heading and each car's boost — is matched against 1,369,736 positions from 1,236 games of Champion 1 and above: the 50 nearest, at most one per game. The table counts how many of those teams conceded within the time we had left; the base rate is the same count over every position.

**Status: computed.** The matching rule is a hypothesis until a human has judged a sample of the pairs (`same_situation` in `pairs.tsv`: is the matched moment really the same situation as ours?). Until then, read the counts as a way to find the moment a goal became likely, not as a verdict on anyone.

**To compare the plays, open [`viewer.html`](https://joshuasandvick.github.io/gangweed_stats/situations/2026-10-07/viewer.html) in Chrome or Edge** (a Markdown preview does not run it). Our play and the matched ones share one clock: pause, step a frame, 0.1 s or 1 s, scrub, change speed, or put ours beside one match. The defending team always defends the bottom goal and is blue; in a matched game the defenders are `def 1`-`def 3` and the attackers `att 1`-`att 3`. Verdicts on each pair can be entered there and exported as `pairs.tsv`.
