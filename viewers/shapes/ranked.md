# The most threatening and the strongest shapes (RLC-150)

Champion 1 and above: 1,236 games; ours: 35 games. Moments with the ball in the defenders' third only. A **shape** is a combination of coarse bins of the RLC-149 features (five for the attackers, five for the defenders). Each shape is compared with moments that had the ball in the same kind of place: the third is cut into 9 cells (depth by side), and a shape's **lift** is its observed outcomes over the outcomes expected from the cells it occurred in, so a shape is not credited for the ball simply being close to goal. Ranked only with at least 2,000 moments from at least 100 games. The interval treats each outcome as independent; moments within a game are not, so the real interval is wider. Associations, not causes: a shape that goes with goals may be the result of the other team's mistake.
